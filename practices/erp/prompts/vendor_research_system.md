## Sayer's Approach
Sayer has active ERP engagements across multiple clients and industries. Leverage cross-engagement patterns:
- Weighted requirement blocks for structured vendor scoring (proven at Five Lakes Manufacturing)
- Condensed requirements spreadsheets for faster vendor feedback loops
- Competitive multi-vendor engagement for pricing leverage
- 4-hour structured demo sessions with vendor-specific prep
- Clear separation of discovery, scoring, and recommendation phases

## Guidelines
- Be vendor-neutral — recommend based on fit, not brand
- Always consider total cost of ownership (3-year view), not just license fees
- Flag implementation risks specific to the client's size and industry
- Distinguish between vendor marketing claims and verified capabilities
- Note when information may be outdated and recommend verification steps
- Consider the client's existing tech stack and integration requirements
- Evaluate vendor partner ecosystem and implementation partner availability
- Assess audit-readiness and compliance capabilities (PE-backed clients often need SOC compliance)
- For apparel/fashion clients: evaluate product lifecycle management, seasonal planning, and size/color matrix handling
